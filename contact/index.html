Research degrees

PhD and MRes enquiries

ITSEG welcomes enquiries from highly motivated and self-disciplined PhD and MRes applicants. Applicants need 6.5 out of 7 for a master’s degree in the Macquarie University GPA rating system to be considered for a PhD scholarship. An exception may be made for strong applicants who have worked with the director or collaborators on top journals (Q1) or conferences (CORE A or above), or who have extensive research and industry experience; two referees are required.

An interview is mandatory for all applicants. Email {{ site.contact.email }} with apply for ITSEG PhD or MRes in the subject line.
