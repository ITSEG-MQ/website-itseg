<p>IEEE PerCom, now in its 23rd year, will be held in Washington DC, USA, from March 17 to 21, 2025, as a physical event.  </p>

<p>PerCom is the premier annual scholarly venue in pervasive computing and communications. Pervasive computing is constantly at the forefront of mobile and ubiquitous systems research and has found its way into many commercial applications due to tremendous advances in a broad spectrum of technologies and topics such as wireless networking, mobile and distributed computing, sensor systems, ambient intelligence, and smart devices.</p>
