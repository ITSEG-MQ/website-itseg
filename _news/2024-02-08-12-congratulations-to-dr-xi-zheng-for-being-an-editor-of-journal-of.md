The Journal on Reliable Intelligent Environments focuses on theoretical developments and lessons learnt on the deployment of Intelligent Environments (IE). The broad areas represented in the journal reflect the fact that reliability of software systems is the result of a number of approaches combined so an overarching principle of our publication will be to be open and flexible to embrace all type of techniques which can increase confidence in Intelligent Environments systems.
