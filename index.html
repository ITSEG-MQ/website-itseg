Our mission

Research for dependable intelligent systems

ITSEG brings together research in trustworthy AI, autonomous systems and software engineering. The group’s work covers cyber-physical systems testing and verification, safety analysis, distributed learning, the Internet of Things, and intelligent connected systems.

Our projects apply these areas to autonomous vehicles, drones, smart farming, e-commerce and other real-world systems.
