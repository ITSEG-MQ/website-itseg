<p>Dr. Yimeng Feng joined the School of Computing at Macquarie University, Sydney, Australia in July 2023 as a Lecturer in Computing (IoT and Networking). Before she joined Macquarie University, she was a Postdoctoral Research Associate at the School of Computer Science and Engineering, University of New South Wales, Sydney, Australia, and a Visiting Research Fellow at the Hawkesbury Institute for the Environment, Western Sydney University, Sydney, Australia. She received her dual Ph.D. degrees from the Faculty of Engineering and IT, the University of Technology Sydney, and the State Key Laboratory of Networking and Switching Technology, Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications.  She received her dual Bachelor's degrees from the Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications and the Queen Mary University of London. She has obtained a high level of achievements, including papers, scholarships, awards, commercialized patents, and professional activities.</p>

<p> Dr. Yimeng Feng's research interests include the Internet of Things, Intelligent Transportation Systems, Service Computing, and Wireless Sensor Network technology. Her professional experiences include mentoring and supervising Ph.D., Master students, and Honours students, as well as serving as a reviewer and TPC member for a number of IEEE/ACM top conferences and journals.
