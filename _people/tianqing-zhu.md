<p>Tianqing Zhu received her BEng and MEng degrees from Wuhan University, China, in 2000 and 2004, respectively, and a Ph.D. degree from Deakin University in Computer Science, Australia, in 2014. Dr. Tianqing Zhu is currently a senior lecturer in the school of software at the University of Technology Sydney, Australia. Before that, she was a lecture in the School of Information Technology, Deakin University, Australia, from 2014 to 2018. Her research interests include privacy-preserving, data mining, and network security.</p>

<p>She has received one ARC Linkage grant (LP170100123) with the title Provable Privacy-preserving Data Sharing System for the Cloud Environment; one ARC Discovery grant (DP190100981) with the title Enhancing privacy preservation in dynamic cyberspace, and four industry grants totaling 120,000 AUD.</p>
