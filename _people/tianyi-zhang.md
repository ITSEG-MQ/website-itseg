<p>Tianyi Zhang is a Tenure-Track Assistant Professor in Computer Science at Purdue University. Prior to that, he was a Postdoctoral Fellow at Harvard University. He worked with Dr. Elena Glassman to build interactive systems that help domain experts explore and make sense of large collections of complex data, e.g., health records and code corpora. He received his Ph.D. from UCLA in 2019 under the supervision of Dr. Miryung Kim.</p>
